# Namibia image provenance

TPTS Grid uses location-specific Namibia photography to establish destination atmosphere. The homepage reconciled on **18 October 2026** assigns **33 unique real-photograph families with zero cross-section repetition**.

The expansion introduced **28 genuinely distinct Wikimedia Commons photographs**. Twenty-seven appear once on the homepage and Hardap Dam is held in the licensed library for a later route or destination surface. Six retained, separately licensed photographs complete the 33 live homepage slots.

This register is part of the product's truth boundary. A photograph is **not** evidence of a TPTS listing, property relationship, room, rate, review, availability or booking status. Property records use a separate factual model and remain unclaimed and non-bookable until their verification, inventory and commercial gates pass.

## Derivative treatment

The production experience serves locally controlled WebP assets rather than loading full-resolution third-party files at runtime. Licensed treatments are limited to what the applicable licence permits:

- full-width, 960 px and 640 px responsive derivatives for every newly sourced photograph;
- responsive crop and focal-position control for desktop, tablet and phone art direction;
- restrained colour, contrast and brightness adjustment for legible interface overlays;
- no change that turns one represented place into another or invents a stay;
- concise creator/source credit beside material editorial use and a complete footer register;
- Creative Commons adaptations retain the creator, source and licence, including share-alike where required.

The photographs are not distributed from this public case-study repository. This repository records provenance and product intent; the canonical production surface carries the rendered derivatives and in-product disclosure.

## Source-level verification

The production test suite treats responsive renditions as one visual family rather than extra images. It verifies that:

- exactly 33 semantic homepage assignments exist;
- each assignment owns a unique source-family ID;
- each photograph has one complete creator, HTTPS source and licence record;
- every referenced responsive candidate is a local WebP file that exists;
- no photograph family is assigned to a second homepage section.

These checks protect provenance and visual variety at source level; they do not replace human review of crop, credit legibility or licence changes.

## Editorial boundaries

The image system is a geographic invitation, not a catalogue:

- real photography shows genuinely different Namibian landscapes, settlements, wildlife, heritage and weather rather than interchangeable travel imagery;
- Country Pulse photographs occupy a dedicated foreground stage and are not hidden beneath the forecast copy;
- captions name the place, creator and source without fabricated stay claims;
- images remain separate from the factual property directory;
- no third-party property image, OTA description, rating, review, logo, price or availability is copied into a public stay record;
- imagery never upgrades an unclaimed place to claimed or bookable status.

Earlier AI-assisted campaign compositions remain archived production history and are not part of the current homepage sequence. If any image is replaced, the replacement must pass the same place, rights, attribution, disclosure and non-misrepresentation review before publication.
